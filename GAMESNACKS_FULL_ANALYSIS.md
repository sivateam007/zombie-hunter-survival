# GameSnacks “Zombie Hunter SURVIVAL” — complete teardown

Source: the shipped Phaser 3 bundle `zh_orig_src/js_game.js` (1,920,197 bytes,
build **1.0.1 / 05.09.2023**, GameSnacks profile `inl-srvvr`) and the eight parsed
stage files in `zh_orig_assets/extracted_levels/`.

Nothing here is from memory. Every table is generated by
`node analysis/full_teardown.js`, which resolves the bundle's named shot
templates (`Object.assign({},BOSS_TOWER_ATTACK1_SHOT,{angleInc:30})`) into
effective values.

| Artifact | What it contains |
|---|---|
| `analysis/teardown.json` | supplies, 30 enemies, 6 bosses with steps, level summaries, sounds, mission types |
| `analysis/weapons_full.md` | verbatim `reset`/`getCount`/`getDamage`/`getRadius`/`getCooldown`/`update` bodies for all 14 weapons |
| `analysis/weapons_summary.json` | cooldowns, base damage, damage multipliers, radii per weapon |
| `analysis/levels_full.md` | **all 370 waves** of all 8 stages, with ms frequencies, caps and boss flags |
| `analysis/missions.json` / `missions.txt` | mission matrix, slot generator, save schema, platform calls |
| `analysis/orig_01…07*.txt` | raw bundle regions (constants, enemies/bosses, levels, skills, weapons, pickups, systems) |

`teardown.json`, `missions.json` and `weapons_summary.json` are the script's
machine-readable outputs. This repo git-ignores `*.json`, so they stay local —
rerunning `node analysis/full_teardown.js` recreates them, and the committed
Markdown/`.txt` extracts above carry the same numbers.

## 3. Player model

`resetAttribs()` sets (verified verbatim):

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| `attack` | POWER skill value (10 at skill level 1) |
| `health` | HEALTH skill value (100 at level 1) |
| `defense` | DEFENSE skill (0 at level 1) |
| `firstaid` | FIRSTAID skill (25 at level 1) |
| `fortune` | FORTUNE skill (0) |
| `learning` | LEARNING skill (0) |
| `speed` | 0.3 px/ms = 300 px/s |
| `pickup_range` | 50 |
| `regen_time` | 5,000 ms |
| all bonuses | 0 (`attack_bonus`, `exp_bonus`, `shot_range_bonus`, `shot_speed_bonus`, `pickup_range_bonus`, `duration_bonus`, `regen_bonus`, `cooldown_bonus`, `speed_bonus`, `cash_bonus`, `health_bonus`) |

Movement: analog vector with dead zone `radius3`; normalized speed scales
`(|v| − radius3) / (2·radius3)`; facing = movement angle. Position is applied
per axis with rejection against the boss-zone fence, static enemies and static
bosses.

Damage taken: `dmg × levelMultiplier × difficultyDMG`, then
`× (1 − defense/100)`, floored at 0, then 3 s invincibility.

XP: `expsToLevelUp = floor(1000 × 1.25^(level−1))`;
`incExps(v) = 3 × floor(v + v·exp_bonus/100 + v·learning/100)`.
(The ×3 is why gold/exp pickups pay triple their face value.)

## 4. Level-up cards, chests, rerolls

- Level-up pauses the scene, hides the stick, pauses anims, opens **3** slots.
- Chests open `ShowSkillSlots(data)` where `data` is the chest size: **1 = bronze,
  3 = silver, 5 = gold**.
- Slot pool (`getDataWeaponsAndSuppliesToUpgrade(b)`): every owned weapon/supply
  whose level `< 5 − b + 1`… precisely, entries with `level >= 5 − b` are removed,
  so a 5-card chest only offers items that can still take 5 levels. Leftover
  slots are filled with coin cards worth **100 / 300 / 500 / 1,000 / 1,500**.
- Reroll prices: level-up `500 × (n+1)` max **5**; chest `1,500 × (n+1)` max **3**.
- Continue price: `2,500 × n` max **2**.
- Meta skills cap at **30**; price ladder `0, 0, 1000, 3000, 6000, 10000, 15000,
  21000, 28000, 36000, 45000 … 435000`.
- Skill values at level 1 → 30: POWER 10 → 880, HEALTH 100 → 4450,
  ARMOR 0 → 43.5, AID 25 → 2350, FORTUNE 0 → 435, LEARNING 0 → 297.5.

## 8. Bosses — 6 types, scripted step lists

Every boss: `attackDelay 1500`, `meleeDelay 200`, chest drop (1/3/5 cards).
Each behaviour step is `{movement, duration:[delay, active, cooldown], speed,
shots[], spawns[]}`.

Movement vocabulary: `CHASE 0, CHARGE 1, ROAM 2, RETREAT 3, TELEPORT 4, SPAWN 5, ATTACK 10`.

### boss_tower — 10,000 hp, static, 9 steps
1–3: 3-way bouncing shot (10 dmg, `bounceBossZn`), delay 1000/0/0, cooldown 1000/1000/2000.
4–6: **12-way ring** of 5 dmg shots (0/30/…/330°), cooldown 1000/1000/2000.
7–9: cluster shard — 20 dmg, fragments, 1300 ms life.

### boss_reptile — 8,000 hp, .25 speed, 5 steps
1. CHASE 6 s at .2 · 2–4. CHARGE ×3 (`[700,3000,100]`, speed .7) ·
5. ATTACK: spawn **4 reptiles** at 350/700/1050/1400 ms.

### boss_soldier — 9,000 hp, .25 speed, 8 steps
1. ROAM 4 s at .2 · 2–5. 5-way fan at −40/−20/0/20/40°, **second identical volley
+500 ms** (10 dmg each) · 6–8. grenade, 30 dmg, radius 80.

### boss_dragon — 7,500 hp, .25 speed, 2 steps
1. CHASE 4 s · 2. **10-shot stream** of 20 dmg projectiles (radius 80),
one every **350 ms** (3,150 ms of fire).

### boss_spider — 8,500 hp, .15 speed, 18 steps
Roam segments at .2–.3 interleaved with: egg mortar (20 dmg, radius 190,
grenade), **spawn 3 spider1**, and a 3-way poison fan at −37/0/+37° whose
projectiles carry a **10 dmg acid fragment** on burst.

### boss_mutant — 9,500 hp, .15 speed, 4 steps
1. CHASE 6 s · 2. CHARGE `[500,2000,50]` speed .7 · 3. **TELEPORT** `[100,1000,100]` ·
4. spawn **4 slime2** at 350 ms intervals.

### Boss zone
`bossZone` is a **rect**, size per stage: 1200×1200 (S1), 1024×1024 (S2),
1200×1000 / 1000×1000 (S3), 1950×1950 (S4), 1600×1600 (S5), 1024×1600 (S6),
3072×3072 (S7–S8). Fence builds on an 80 px grid (`BOSS_ZONE_GRID_SIZE`), the
player collides with it, **all normal enemies at the boss are killed**, the
player is moved inside, and boss music starts. Boss waves fire at **300 s** and
**600 s**, each lasting 30 s. Stages 4–8 send **two bosses per zone**.

Frequencies are authored as `LOW/MID/HI` = **700 / 350 / 100 ms**. Every stage's

### Wave scheduler

```
updateInactiveWave(w):
  if startTime - time > 3000: return
  if 0 < startTime - time and w.bossZone: banner "INCOMING_BOSS" 3 s
  else activateWave(w)

activateWave(w):
  w.active = true
  if w.bossZone: build rect fence, killAllEnemiesAtBoss(),
                 movePlayerFromOuterLimits(), spawnBosses(), play music_boss
  if time >= end: deactivate

updateActiveWave(w):
  if w.bosses and bosses alive: return      // clock and normal spawns freeze
  w.delay -= dt
  if w.delay <= 0: w.delay = w.frequency; spawnEnemy(w.enemy)

spawnEnemy(type):
  if count(type) >= w.max: destroyFarthest(type)   // recycle at cap
  place at half-diagonal × 1.3 around player (× 2.2 near map edges)
  miniboss waves show "INCOMING_MINIBOSS" for 3 s
```

## 12. Gap analysis against Meow Zombie Hunter

Verified counts on our side: **28 enemy types, 7 bosses, 20 stages, 40 weapons
(20 base + 20 evolved), 14 supplies**.

### Missing or different (ranked)

1. **Boss AI.** Originals run 2–18 step scripts with charges (700/500 ms windups),
   teleports, add spawns and timed volleys (5-way double volleys, 12-way rings,
   10-shot streams at 350 ms, egg mortars). Ours walk at the hunter and fire one
   straight shot. Our extra `boss_fighter` has no original counterpart.
2. **Boss arena.** Rect fence of 1000–3072 px on an 80 px grid, player collision,
   kill-all-at-boss, player confined, per-boss bars, boss music. Ours only freezes
   the clock and can be walked out of.
3. **Death hazards.** Acid pools on six enemy types (toxic1/2, slime1/2, both
   miniboss toxins), the manager's briefcase, and corpse splitting (slime2 → 2
   slime1, slug → 2 slime2).
4. **Chest semantics.** 1/3/5-card chests with a separate `1500 × (n+1)` reroll
   capped at 3. Ours always shows 3 cards and shares the level-up reroll.
5. **Food pickup** (heals 100) and the bomb formula (`10 × attack`, 900 px).
6. **Magnet pulls gems only**; ours vacuums every drop.
7. **Player collision with `mass` 1/2/5 bodies** — a miniboss should shove you.
8. **Wave ceilings.** Original caps reach 300 concurrent at 100 ms cadence and
   recycle the farthest body. Ours deliberately opens at 6→27 and ramps to 88.
9. **Victory beat.** 5 s hold before results.
10. **Music.** Three tracks with per-track volumes; we ship SFX only.
11. **Meta**: 15 mission types with reward tiers, 168 h coffer, suspended runs,
    rewarded-ad rerolls.

### Where we are ahead

Magazines + manual tactical reload (original has neither), jetpack with ignition
cost, dash with i-frames, 20 base weapons vs 14, 35 survivors with powers, store
and character upgrades, daily rewards and redeem codes, 20 stages vs 8, endless
mode, 19-district city with weather, leaderboard with sanitising, and the
knockback/shockwave/vignette feedback layer.

### Suggested order

1. Boss step interpreter (`movement` + `duration [delay, active, cooldown]` +
   `shots[]`/`spawns[]`) and per-boss step lists.
2. Boss-zone rect + fence collision + kill-all-at-boss + per-boss bars.
3. `deadshot` pools and `deadspawn` splits.
4. Chest card counts and their own reroll sink.
5. Food, bomb formula, magnet semantics, player mass collision.
6. 5 s victory beat and a music bed.

## 13. Limits of this analysis

This is a static teardown of code, data tables and eight parsed level files; I
did not play the shipped build. Presentation details (animation timings, UI copy)
were read from the bundle and its translation keys, and the behaviour interpreter
choices above are derived from the data structures rather than observed in play.
Everything numeric is traceable to `analysis/teardown.json` or the generated
`weapons_*` / `levels_full.md` files.
