HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • ThumbstickMove — your hunter aims where they walk
  • WASD / ArrowsMove on a keyboard; the mouse is the crosshair
  • GamepadLeft stick moves, right stick aims, A flies, B rolls
  • Auto-fireWeapons shoot on their own
  • ROLL / ShiftCombat roll (i-frames)
  • RELOAD / RStart a ring reload, then tap again as a round crosses the notch to seat it
  • II / Esc / PPause

Drag the thumbstick on the left to move. ROLL and RELOAD sit on the right, under your thumb.

SURVIVAL RULES

  • Clear 20 waves to finish a stage. Survive wave 20 and the next stage deploys automatically with your run carried over. Boss zones freeze the clock until the bosses die.
  • Keep moving. The SIEGE meter fills while you stand still; once it fills, extra zombies burst in from every side. Camping is not a strategy here.
  • Level up for a 3-card offer: a new weapon, a weapon level, or a supply.
  • Supplies stack five levels and let a level-6 weapon evolve into its real form.
  • Scrap is dropped by the dead. Spend it on the six permanent skills in the safehouse.
  • Rolling is a fast reposition — it slips through gaps but does not make you invulnerable.
  • Level-up cards carry a 10-second countdown: take an offer before it ends or the first one is auto-picked.
  • HOT MAG — seat 4 of the 6 ring rounds perfectly for +20% damage for 5 seconds.
  • Jetpack — hold JET (or Space) to fly over bites. Fuel recharges on the ground.
  • Boss zones seal the street, clear the horde and put a fence around the fight. Learn each boss's ring, fan, stream or summon.
  • Ranged enemies (cops, turrets, assaults) shoot back. Break line or close in fast.
