HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • ThumbstickMove — your hunter aims where they walk
  • WASD / ArrowsMove on a keyboard; the mouse is the crosshair
  • GamepadLeft stick moves, right stick aims, A flies, B rolls
  • Auto-fireWeapons shoot on their own
  • ROLL / ShiftCombat roll (i-frames)
  • RELOAD / RReload early — or wait for the click
  • II / Esc / PPause

Drag the thumbstick on the left to move. ROLL and RELOAD sit on the right, under your thumb.

SURVIVAL RULES

  • Survive 20 minutes to clear a stage. Boss zones freeze the clock until the bosses die.
  • Level up for a 3-card offer: a new weapon, a weapon level, or a supply.
  • Supplies stack five levels and let a level-6 weapon evolve into its real form.
  • Scrap is dropped by the dead. Spend it on the six permanent skills in the safehouse.
  • Rolling makes you briefly untouchable — use it to slip out of a bite.
  • Force field cells arm a 60-second shield. The HUD counts it down.
  • Jetpack — hold JET (or Space) to fly over bites. Fuel recharges on the ground.
  • Ranged enemies (cops, turrets, assaults) shoot back. Break line or close in fast.
